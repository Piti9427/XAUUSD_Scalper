# Selected26b8 trial-and-error tuning loop (2026-11-11)

Owner goal: keep trying cases until results beat native, judged by higher profit per month or more
profitable months, and root-cause every failure. Artifacts (gitignored):
`runs/c22-26b8-tuning-loop-2026-11-11/`, containing `protocol.json` (sha256 `86cecb6b…fc60`),
`loopeng.py`, `loop.py`, `loop-result.json`, `rc_confirm.json` and `posthoc.json`.

## Setup
- Engine: composed in-memory patch. It covers tick BE at 5 s polling, the side-aware lock and the existing hooks.
  With every knob off it equals native in all 14 segment × view cells (`parity.json`).
- Accounts: an independent $313 account per year, with indicators prepared once per tape. Warm-up months are excluded.
- Selection years: 2021, 2023, 2024-09..2025-12. Confirmation years: 2020, 2022, 2024-01..08, 2026.
  Every year had already been seen.
- Views: base+swap and stress+swap. Only runtime-feasible knobs (fixed 0.01, 10% cap).
- Gate (each view) vs native:
  - pooled monthly mean ≥ native + $1;
  - % profitable months ≥ native;
  - worst month ≥ native − $5;
  - beats native by more than $5 in ≥ 2 of 3 selection years.

## Loop result: 4 rounds, 49 cases, final CONFIRM_FAIL

| Round | Parent → winner | Selection edge ($/month, min of views) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | B2 (tick BE BUY) fails stress → `B2+maxloss_1` | +1.52 |
| 2 | `+asia_buyblock` | +4.86 |
| 3 | `+sell_rr_1.5` | +6.30 |
| 4 | `+sell_pa_0.8` | +7.05 |

Final candidate on the confirmation years (run once):
- base: $11.96 vs native $12.37 per month;
- stress: $7.07 vs $0.80, but profitable months fell from 46.3% to 39.0%.

Result: CONFIRM_FAIL. The candidate cut bad-year losses (2020 −126 vs −183, 2022 +76 vs −24,
2024a −74 vs −174) but gave up 2026 trend profit (+614 vs +887).

Root causes:
- Round 1, buylock 2R→1R (rejected): it gained +$225 on BUY trades that had been closing at BE, but
  8 BUY TP trades were locked out (−$193), and stress profitable months fell.
- Round 2, BUY tick BE at 0.75R (rejected): 8 BUY TPs were scratched to BE (−$265) against 16 SL
  trades turned into BE (+$140).
- Confirmation 2026, final vs native: maxloss_1 removed 21 SELL TP trades (−$968) and 4 BUY TPs
  (−$301), offset by 40 skipped SL trades (+$1,211). SELL BE at 0.8R turned 11 SELL TPs into BE (−$349).
  In trend years, the trade after a day's first loss is often a winner.
- Leave-one-out (diagnostic on confirmation data): stacking sell_rr_1.5, sell_pa_0.8 and asia_buyblock on
  top of maxloss_1 lowered the base confirmation mean. Removing any one of them raises it. The greedy loop
  overfit by stacking knobs that each won small amounts on the selection years.

## Simplest candidate: B2 + maxloss_1 (POST-HOC, confirmation data already seen)

`max_daily_losses=1`, together with BUY tick BE at 1R polled every 5 s. It passed the round-1 selection
gate (edge +1.52) but lost the greedy race to stacked knobs.

| All 7 years | native base | candidate base | native stress | candidate stress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mean / month | $13.89 | **$17.45** | $4.93 | **$9.00** |
| Profitable months | 46.8% | **53.2%** | 45.6% | **50.6%** |
| Worst month | −121.81 | −121.86 | −135.05 | −130.79 |

- Confirmation years alone: base $16.19 vs $12.37, and profitable months 53.7% vs 46.3%.
- Trade-off: lower profit in strong trend years (2026 alone with maxloss_1: +707 vs +887) and smaller
  losses in range years.

Caveats:
- This result is post-hoc, so it is not clean evidence.
- `max_daily_losses` is part of the 26b8 research `Rules`. Changing it gives a new research RulesID and a
  new contract, plus cost-generation review and owner approval.
- Forward observation is the only remaining clean test. "Profit every month" was not reached by any case.

## Loop v2: side and regime loss rules (protocol2.json, all 7 years, ≤2 knobs)

Derived from the v1 root cause (maxloss_1 blocks trend-year winners). Gate for each view: all-7 monthly mean
≥ native + $1, % profitable ≥ native, worst ≥ native − $5, and native beaten by more than $5 in ≥ 4/7 years.
Six of 10 cases pass.

| Case | Edge $/mo (min view) | Base mean / % +months / worst | 2021 | 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| native | — | 13.89 / 46.8 / −121.81 | −213 | +887 |
| **B2+maxloss_1** | **+3.56** | 17.45 / 53.2 / −121.86 | −119 | +763 |
| B2+side1 | +2.03 | 16.30 / 55.7 / −121.86 | −109 | +735 |
| B2+range1 | +1.52 | 15.82 / 49.4 / **−85.32** | −188 | **+910** |
| B2+buy_after_loss | +1.34 | 15.23 / 51.9 / −121.86 | −177 | +769 |

Root cause of the best case (B2+maxloss_1 vs native, 2026, base):
- It still loses 16 SELL TPs (−$965) against skipped losers worth +$967.
- Risk-cap rejections rise from 7 to 53. Lower balance after the skipped winners means the 10% cap
  ($31 at $313) rejects more 2.5ATR stops at 2026 gold prices.
- So part of the trend-year give-back is the cap path, not the loss rule itself.

New finding:
- `range1` (stop after the first loss only when |h4dist| is below the signal median) has the best worst month
  (−85 vs −122) and keeps 2026 profit (+910).
- It costs about $75–100 in the 2021 and 2023 range years.

Verdict:
- B2+maxloss_1 remains the most robust candidate and passes the stricter all-7-year gate. Every number is
  in-sample on already-seen years, so forward observation is required.
- The next structural lever is the risk-cap binding at high gold prices (2.5ATR stop > $31 cap at $313).
  The earlier stop-shrink tests failed at $200. A re-test at $313 has not been run.
