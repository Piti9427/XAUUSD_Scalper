contains no40% portfolio breaker. Before adding one, define H as a validated,
persisted **equity** high-water for an explicit cohort, its observation frequency,
cashflow treatment and restart/resume policy. Balance peak is not equity peak;
history before cohort start must not silently become that cohort's reference.
Unknown deposits/withdrawals/credits require reconciliation, not an invented peak.

**P1: prospective DD entry floor.** While flat, additionally require
`E − R > 0.60 × H` in its isolated form. It avoids knowingly adding planned risk below the40%
floor. It does not close an existing trade and cannot guarantee future DD. A flat

is essential. Never reset H on restart or to make recovery appear complete.

**P2: continuously observed DD breaker.** At E≤0.60H, initiate an owned close and
persist halt. This changes exits, requires the same H definition and explicit

guarantee DD≤40% through price gaps, delayed polling, market closure or outages.
