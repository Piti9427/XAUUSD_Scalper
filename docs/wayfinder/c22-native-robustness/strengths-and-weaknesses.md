The 2025 stress ledger contains 55 BUYs that reached at least +1R and later closed at an updated stop, for −$28.53 combined after costs. Use Exness bid/ask timestamps to measure when +1R was first observable, the next completed M15, whether the revised stop was executable, and how much profit was captured on each TP or stop. Compare against earlier periods and account for costs and remaining exposure to the initial stop. Keep the cold SL and risk-cap amplification work in the backlog since it concerns a different stage of the trade.
