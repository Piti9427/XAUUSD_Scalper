XAUUSD Scalper Architecture

System architecture and pipeline topology: Ingress feeds, Pure B1 strategy detection, strict 10% risk gate, and decoupled execution outbox.

Tier 01 · Ingress & Sensors

Market Feeds & Filters

  • mt5_connector.py — Real-time M15 bar polling and live bid/ask spread monitoring directly from MetaTrader 5 API.
  • news_service.py — Forex macroeconomic calendar guard; blocks entries during high-impact USD events (NFP, CPI, FOMC).
  • webhook_bridge.py — Optional secondary HTTP listener for TradingView alerts and dry-run webhook verification.

Tier 02 · Strategy & Risk Boundary

Pure B1 & Risk Invariants

  • scripts/strategy.py — Target single-position Donchian breakout (20-period) with RSI 30–70 guard and EMA 50 trend filter. Dynamic 6.25 ATR TP / 2.5 ATR SL.
  • CORE_STRATEGY.md — Single source of truth. Zero basket recovery, zero martingale. Strict 10% equity risk cap per trade.
  • main.py / smc_main.py — Alert loop and simulation runner with hard 40% ($80) daily loss circuit breaker.

Tier 03 · Outbox & Safety Harness

Execution, Alerts & Evidence

  • b1_outbox.py — Decoupled staging outbox that enforces broker-reconciliation before order firing. Live remains NO-GO.
  • notifier.py & gws_logger.py — Telegram bot sending structured trade levels with Google Workspace audit log sheet.
  • tests/ & data/ — Deterministic unit test suite and 6-month Exness tick replay dataset (reproducible verification contract).
